🔗 Binary Relation Inspector

Formal Definitions:
• Reflexive: ∀x ∈ V, (x,x) ∈ R
• Symmetric: (x,y) ∈ R ⇒ (y,x) ∈ R
• Transitive: (x,y) ∈ R ∧ (y,z) ∈ R ⇒ (x,z) ∈ R
• Equivalence: Reflexive ∧ Symmetric ∧ Transitive
